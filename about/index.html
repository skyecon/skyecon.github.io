Technology work with a clear boundary and a checkable result.

SKYECON is the public technology brand of Skyecon Contracting Ltd., a British Columbia company. The immediate focus is bounded operational software for public-sector work.

Company provenance

Practical systems judgment, applied to software.

SKYECON draws on a British Columbia background spanning early computing, aircraft maintenance, technical direction, business ownership, and hands-on delivery. That path established a practical view of systems: visible results depend on the interfaces, authority, and operating conditions beneath them.

The company is building trust in increments. Define the outcome. Bound the work. Complete it against explicit acceptance criteria. Document what was delivered and what remains outside scope. Earn the next responsibility through evidence.

Operating principles

What the work should make visible.

01

Outcome before activity

Define what must work before selecting tasks, technologies, or appearances.

02

Conditions before promises

Inspect the environment, dependencies, authority, and failure consequences first.

03

Root cause before workaround

Repair the mechanism where possible instead of stacking another temporary layer.

04

Evidence before claim

Distinguish development proof, submitted work, acceptance, release, and real-world outcome.

05

Handoff without ambiguity

Document decisions, interfaces, limits, and responsibilities so the work can continue responsibly.
