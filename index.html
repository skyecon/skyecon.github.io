03 / Priority opportunity

B.C. government is a priority market.

Government organizations face consequential operational problems where privacy, records, accessibility, authorization, review, and defensible handoff matter. SKYECON brings first-hand problem knowledge of this environment, making it an important market for commissioned systems and future products.

The same operating discipline applies to commercial organizations with comparable operational constraints. Customer ownership, licensing, and deployment control are decided explicitly rather than assumed.

For an initial discussion, describe the outcome, present constraint, and acceptance test. Do not send credentials, protected records, private source code, recordings, or sensitive data.
