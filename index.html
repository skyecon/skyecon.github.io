03 / British Columbia

Start with a bounded outcome. Earn the next responsibility.

SKYECON is building toward public-sector work where privacy, records, accessibility, authorization, review, and defensible handoff matter. The immediate offer is deliberately narrow: complete a defined piece of operational software against explicit acceptance criteria.

Customer ownership, open-source delivery, or customer-controlled deployment can be supported where the procurement and technical requirements call for them. The contract decides ownership; the work should remain understandable.

For an initial discussion, describe the outcome, present constraint, and acceptance test. Do not send credentials, protected records, private source code, recordings, or sensitive data.
